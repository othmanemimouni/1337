
## Grademe.fr

**This repository also contains exercises and challenges from Grademe.fr, used throughout my practice and preparation for evaluations.**

**The goal is not to memorize solutions or simply make tests pass. The goal is to understand the problem, write your own solution, and develop the ability to solve it independently.**

> **Don't cheat your exercises.**

**Try the exercise yourself first. If you get stuck or finish it, use the solutions in this repository as a reference to compare your approach, understand different implementations, and learn from your mistakes.**

```text
Try → Solve → Test → Debug → Compare → Understand
```

**Your solution. Your reasoning. Your progress.**
